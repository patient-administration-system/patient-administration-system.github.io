This is the closest commercial comparator, because Dedalus sells a patient administration system on its own. It describes ORBIS PAS as [a next-generation patient administration system](https://www.dedalus.com/uki/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/ORBIS-PAS-Flyer-for-web.pdf) for the UK and Ireland, and as a first step towards a full electronic record. Dedalus [launched it across both countries](https://www.dedalus.com/uki/media/news/dedalus-launches-orbis-pas-across-uk-and-ireland/).

## Comparison

| | Dedalus ORBIS | This project |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Patient administration, with a route to a full record | Patient administration only |
| **Clinical record** | Available through ORBIS | No, by design |
| **Built on** | Dedalus's NHS and Irish health service experience, by its own account | New code, not yet proven in live use |
| **Source code** | Not public, as far as we can find | Public, open-source licences |
| **Supplier support** | Yes | None yet |

## Suitability

### When Dedalus ORBIS is suitable

You need a supported patient administration system, with a supplier and a route to a full record.

### When this project may be suitable

You want to inspect and change how reminders, replies and fairness measures work.
