This website keeps a few choices you make in your own browser, so that the next page you open, and your next visit, look the way you asked. This page says what they are, where they are kept, and why.

## What is kept

Nothing is kept until you make a choice in the bar at the top of the page. Each item records only which option you picked. None of them identifies you, and none is sent to us.

| Your choice | Where it is kept | For how long | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Theme (the site's colours) | Your browser's local storage (`pas-site-theme`) and a cookie of the same name | Until you clear it; the cookie for at most 400 days | So that every page opens in the theme you chose. Keeping it in both means a choice survives if one of them is cleared. |
| Time zone | Local storage (`pas-site-time-zone`) | Until you clear it | So that times are shown in your time zone |
| Text size | Local storage (`pas-site-text-size`) | Until you clear it | So that text stays the size you chose |
| Language | Not kept. It is part of the page's address, for example `/cy-gb/` | Not applicable | So that a link opens in the language it was shared in |

Your browser sends the theme cookie with each request for a page of this site, as it does with any cookie. This site is made of static files, so nothing on our side reads it.

## What is not kept

- There are no accounts, no sign-in and no forms.
- There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and nothing follows you to other sites.
- Search runs in your browser, over a copy of the site's index. Your search words appear in the page's address, as they would in any link.
- Sharing opens your own email program, or copies the link, and keeps nothing.

## Who else is involved

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives each request, as any web host does, including your IP address, and handles it under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changing or removing your choices

Choose again at any time in the bar at the top of each page. To remove every choice, clear this site's data in your browser's settings. The site then uses its defaults again.
