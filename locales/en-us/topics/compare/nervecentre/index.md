Nervecentre supplies an electronic patient record to NHS trusts. Mid Yorkshire signed [a ten-year contract for its cloud-based system](https://www.midyorks.nhs.uk/news/mid-yorkshire-signs-epr-contract-with-nervecentre-4506). At Harrogate and District it [replaces many older systems and paper processes](https://www.hdft.nhs.uk/news/harrogate-and-district-nhs-foundation-trust-successfully-launches-phase-two-of-its-electronic-patient-record-system/). Two Derbyshire trusts scored it highest [among seven providers](https://uhdb.nhs.uk/latest-news/new-electronic-patient-record-system-one-step-closer-as-preferred-supplier-identified-16684).

## Comparison

| | Nervecentre | This project |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A cloud-based electronic patient record | Patient administration only |
| **Clinical record** | Yes | No, by design |
| **In use with real patients** | Yes, in NHS trusts | Not yet |
| **Source code** | Not public, as far as we can find | Public, open-source licenses |
| **Supplier support** | Yes | None yet |

## Suitability

### When Nervecentre is suitable

You need a supported electronic patient record for a hospital trust.

### When this project may be suitable

Your interest is in reminders, replies and fairness measures that you can inspect and change. It is not a clinical system.
