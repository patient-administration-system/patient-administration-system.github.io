This is the most relevant comparison for NHS Wales. WelshPAS, also called WPAS, is [the digital system used by most hospitals in Wales to book patients into clinics](https://www.gov.wales/written-statement-update-progress-cancer-informatics-programme). Digital Health and Care Wales calls it [the largest IT system in NHS Wales](https://dhcw.nhs.wales/news/latest-news/welsh-patient-administration-system-links-up-north-wales/), handling more than 2.6 billion transactions a year.

This project is independent of Digital Health and Care Wales. It is not a replacement for WelshPAS, and no such decision has been made.

## Comparison

| | WelshPAS | This project |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The national patient administration system in live use | A patient administration project, not in live use |
| **In use with real patients** | Yes, across Wales | Not yet |
| **Scale** | More than 2.6 billion transactions a year | Not proven at scale |
| **Identity data** | Records patient information | Holds opaque identifiers only |
| **Source code** | Not public, as far as we can find | Public, open-source licenses |
| **Run by** | Digital Health and Care Wales | An open-source project |

## Suitability

### When WelshPAS is suitable

Services run in NHS Wales today. It is the system in place.

### When this project may be suitable

You are researching reminders, replies and fairness measures, or exploring ideas that could inform future work.
