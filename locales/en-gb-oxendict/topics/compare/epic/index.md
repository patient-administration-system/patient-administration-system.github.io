Epic is a US company that supplies electronic patient records. King's College Hospital and Guy's and St Thomas' went live with it together in October 2023, [replacing hundreds of separate IT systems](https://www.kch.nhs.uk/news/new-electronic-patient-record-goes-live/). Guy's and St Thomas' also planned [a patient portal for records and booking](https://guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/news/new-digital-partnership-improve-patient-care).

## Comparison

| | Epic | This project |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A whole-hospital electronic patient record | Patient administration only |
| **Clinical record** | Yes | No, by design |
| **In use with real patients** | Yes, including in NHS trusts | Not yet |
| **Source code** | Not public, as far as we can find | Public, open-source licences |
| **Supplier support** | Yes | None yet |

## Suitability

### When Epic is suitable

You need one proven system for clinical care and administration, and your organization can fund a large programme.

### When this project may be suitable

You want to read or change the code, study fair access to appointments, or contribute. It cannot replace an electronic patient record.
